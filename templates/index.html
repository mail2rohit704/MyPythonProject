🌱 Pea Seed Health Prediction

ML-powered system to detect and classify individual pea seeds as Healthy or Unhealthy for smarter crop management

🔍 How It Works

Our machine learning model analyzes your uploaded seed image and automatically labels each seed as Healthy or Unhealthy. This helps you quickly identify problem areas and take timely preventive action.

  • Automatic seed-by-seed classification (Healthy / Unhealthy)
  • Visual labels drawn on the seeds for easy inspection
  • Early detection of unhealthy seeds for better crop yield
  • Downloadable health reports with classification results
  • Responsive, mobile-friendly interface

Simply upload a clear image of your pea seeds and get instant results!
